Bíblia IPM is a Portuguese Bible reader with a hymnal, songs, documents and church bulletin.

Read offline, compare translations, save favorites, notes and highlights, and search across the available content.

Set a daily reading goal or a completion date, explicitly mark finished chapters and enable an optional local reminder.

Share and import JSON backups and separate church bulletins. Text-to-speech uses the device engine; offline voices must be installed in the operating system.
